Questions

Does Tidy read my email?

No. Tidy only changes which buttons Gmail shows in your own browser. It never reads, stores or sends the content of your emails. See the privacy policy.

Will I lose any Gmail features?

No. Tidy hides buttons; it doesn't turn anything off. Your inbox tabs, package cards, Smart Compose and everything else keep working.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

The scrolling fix stays free forever. The other switches lock until you unlock them with a one-time $3.99 payment. Your settings are saved, so everything comes back the way you had it.

I paid on another computer. How do I unlock it here?

Click the Tidy icon in Chrome, choose "Already paid? Log in," and enter the email you used at checkout. You'll get a login link — no second payment.

How are payments handled?

Securely by Stripe through ExtensionPay. We never see your card number.

Does it work in other languages or browsers?

Tidy works in Google Chrome (and Chrome-based browsers like Edge) with Gmail set to English.

Something isn't hidden or doesn't work.

Gmail changes its layout from time to time. Email lbell@awata-app.com and we'll fix it in the next update — updates install automatically.
