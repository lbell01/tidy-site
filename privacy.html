Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version
  • Tidy never reads, stores or sends your emails.
  • Your switch settings stay in your own browser.
  • If you buy, Stripe and ExtensionPay handle payment. We never see your card number.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for ads.

Tidy for Gmail ("Tidy") is a Chrome extension that hides optional buttons in Gmail and keeps your email list scrolled to the top when you switch views. This policy explains what information Tidy handles.

Your email stays private

Tidy never reads, copies, stores or sends the content of your emails, contacts or attachments. It only changes how Gmail's buttons are displayed in your own browser.

Your settings

Your on/off choices, your free-trial end date and whether you've purchased are saved using Chrome's built-in storage, so they stay the same between visits. We do not receive your settings.

Purchases

If you unlock all features, payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. During checkout you give your email address and payment details directly to them. We never see or store your card number.

ExtensionPay keeps your email address and purchase status so Tidy can confirm you've paid, including when you log in on another computer. Tidy contacts ExtensionPay only to check that status.

See the Stripe Privacy Policy and ExtensionPay's website for how they handle your information.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as needed to process your purchase.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
